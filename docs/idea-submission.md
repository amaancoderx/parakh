# Idea submission (Builders 2026, Step 5)

Deliberately broad: it names no models and no mechanism.

**Theme:** Build Challenging (Voice AI for India)

**Project name:** Parakh

## What are you building?

Parakh (परख, the jeweller's test for real gold) is a trust layer for voice AI. It sits between any speech model and the assistant that acts on it, and tests every important word the way a jeweller tests gold: was it actually heard, or does it only look right?

It is for any product where an Indian user's voice turns into action: calls, payments, bookings, support. Today's models hand over their best guess with full confidence. Parakh treats what the model heard as a claim, weighs it against what is known about the person speaking, and lets through only what holds up. What does not hold up is flagged instead of acted on.

When it works, you see the same recording on a base model and on Parakh: what changed, why it changed, and what Parakh refused to guess.

## What exact problem are you solving?

Voice AI in India does not fail on the easy words. It fails on the words that carry the action: who, how much, where, when. And it fails silently, sounding exactly as sure as when it is right.

Anyone whose assistant has called the wrong person, noted the wrong amount or mangled their own name knows what follows: after one such mistake you stop trusting it with anything that matters. A bigger model does not remove this, and giving it more context creates a new failure, where it starts hearing what it expects instead of what was said.

The problem we are solving is trust: a voice AI that can tell what it heard from what it assumed, in the way Indians actually speak on the phone.
