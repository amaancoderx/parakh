Who it is for

Anywhere an Indian voice turns into an action.

A misheard name sends the order to the wrong person. A misheard amount moves the wrong money. Parakh makes the words that carry the action trustworthy.

Call assistants

Call screening and call summaries where the caller's name, company and amount have to be right before anyone reads them.

Voice agents

Booking, support and ordering agents that take action on what they hear. Parakh returns a draft at once and the checked words a second later.

Orders & payments

Trade calls, collections and payments, where “chaalis ya bayalis” and “Mohan or Mohammad” decide what actually happens.

How it works

It never guesses a name. It proves it, or it asks.

No model is retrained. Parakh is a checking layer over the speech models you already use.

01 · Listen

Three models hear the call

Sarvam Saaras v4, v3 and OpenAI hear the same audio side by side, with no hints that could bias them.

02 · Doubt

Find where they disagree

Where the models write different things, something unusual was said. Numbers are compared by value, however they were written.

03 · Check

Match against people you know

Names you have confirmed that sound like the doubtful words, across Hindi and Roman script. The audio has to back the name.

04 · Decide

Fix, or ask once

Proven names are fixed, numbers become digits by rule, and anything uncertain becomes one tap-to-answer question.
